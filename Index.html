Tuition & Fees

These are the terms shown to families in the Parent Portal.

$45 annual registration fee per student plus an annual tuition of $2,600 for the 37-week program. Each additional child in a family receives a 10% discount on tuition only — $2,340 a year — never on the registration fee.

Tuition is billed in five two-month periods, each payable before the period begins. The registration fee is collected with the family's first payment.

Registering Mid-Year

A family joining partway through the year does not pay for classes already gone, and the scheduled payments stay at their standard amounts. The system prorates the tuition to the weeks remaining, subtracts the billing dates still ahead, and collects the difference at registration along with the registration fee.

Example — one child joining in week 3
1. Tuition for 35 of 37 weeks ($2,600 ÷ 37 × 35)$2,459.46
2. Four billing dates still ahead (4 × $520)− $2,080.00
3. Balance due at registration $379.46
   plus the annual registration fee+ $45.00
Collected at registration $424.46

The same method applies to siblings, using their discounted figures ($2,340 a year, $468 per period).
